# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Pet owners (cat and dog, with more species planned) who are away from home for any reason — work hours, errands, or travel — and want live visual and interactive access to their pet in the moment, not just after-the-fact footage.

## Product Purpose

Nuzzle pairs a live HD camera with NuzzlePal, a proprietary AI companion robot, so an owner can watch, talk to, and play with their pet from anywhere, with the AI keeping the pet company and engaged between the owner's own check-ins.

## Positioning

Positioned against passive pet cameras (Furbo-style treat-cams that only stream video): NuzzlePal is an AI companion that actively interacts — talks, plays, moves around — rather than just recording. The mechanism a passive-camera competitor can't truthfully copy is active, in-between-check-ins engagement with the pet, not just remote viewing.

## Operating Context

NuzzlePal is a small, two-wheeled home companion robot (cat/small-dog scale) placed in the home. Owners access it through a companion app with Live / Talk / Insights views. Plans and hardware behavior are species-specific (separate Cat and Dog offerings, plus a Future Pets waitlist tier for rabbits/birds/etc.), selected via a pricing wheel that also includes a one-time Bonus discount-voucher wedge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This is a demo/portfolio site, not a real company — there is no backend. "Choose plan," "Join the Waitlist," and "Copy code" only show a toast or use the Clipboard API; nothing persists across reloads or submits anywhere. Pricing, stats (e.g. "11,400+ homes"), and testimonials are explicitly illustrative placeholder content, not real figures. The bonus voucher's promo code and issued/expiry dates are real, freshly computed values, but the discount isn't wired to any checkout.

## Brand Commitments

Product name "Nuzzle"; the robot is named "NuzzlePal." The FAQ is deliberately direct about how the AI is trained (public pet-behavior footage and other public data, fine-tuned per-pet with owner consent) and states plainly that NuzzlePal is a companion, not a substitute for veterinary care or an in-person sitter — this honest framing must be preserved, not softened into vague "proprietary AI" marketing language. Every subject photo/video (dogs, cats, the robot itself) must be real, freely-licensed footage — never an illustration or AI-generated image/video — with narrow, already-documented exceptions for interactive SVG overlays, the roaming mascot icons, and decorative background art (see the repo's own CLAUDE.md for the exact boundaries).

## Evidence on Hand

No real business data exists for this fictional company — pricing, stats, and testimonials are illustrative placeholders, stated as such in the repo. Real photo/video assets are sourced from Pexels (Pexels License; see README credits); decorative background icons are CC0-licensed from SVG Repo.

## Product Principles

- Interaction over observation — the product's edge is a companion that actively engages the pet, not a passive camera feed.
- Honesty about the AI, stated in the open — the FAQ says plainly how the AI is trained rather than leaning on vague "proprietary AI" language.
- Real photography only — every subject photo/video is genuine, freely-licensed footage; illustration stays confined to narrow interactive or decorative iconography.
- Species-specific by design — cats, dogs, and future pets each get distinct hardware behavior, accent colors, and plan tiers rather than one-size-fits-all.
- Preserve function through every redesign — visual passes must never change an element's id/class/data-* attributes or break existing interactivity.
