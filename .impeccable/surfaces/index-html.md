Also folds in the interaction fixes from the 2026-09-23 critique (`.impeccable/critique/2026-09-23T06-48-08Z__index-html.md`) where they intersect visual chrome, per explicit user instruction: demote the plans wheel out of the primary purchasing path, remove the forced spin-delay tax on direct tab clicks, keep roaming mascots clear of the hero on mobile, morph the hamburger icon, fix the stale NuzzlePal photo credit, and delete the kicker labels / restructure the icon-tile feature cards the detector flagged. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Editorial spec-sheet" — real photography and huge condensed poster type carry the page, not glow/gradient chrome. Refuses the neon-dark-AI-SaaS default this project has now shipped three times (soft-pastel → brutalist → dark-cosmic were all still "look how techy this is" costumes; this is the first pass to look like a print/product studio instead of a UI kit).

OWN-WORLD: warm near-white paper `#F1EFEB` / near-black ink `#0B0B0A`, `#E5E2DC` and `#DAD6CE` for stepped card/section surfaces. Bebas Neue (condensed, all-caps, tight tracking) for every heading at every scale, from the hero's giant headline down to card h3s. Manrope stays for body copy, nav, and buttons. Space Mono stays for timestamps and small technical micro-labels (now doing double duty as literal spec-sheet callouts on the NuzzlePal photo). Buttons are small-radius solid near-black fills with white text — flat, no gradient, no pill shape, no glow shadow. Species colors survive only as small flat muted tag fills (cat `#B23E6B`, dog `#2E5AA8`, future `#3D7A5C`, bonus `#A87C2E`) on pills/badges, never as backgrounds, gradients, or glows. Small black caption chips with a folded-corner mark sit over real photos (hero card, service photos, testimonial avatars), replacing the old glassy chip convention. Zero blur, zero glass, zero starfield, zero colored glow shadow anywhere.

STORY: visitor lands on a huge, confident headline beside the real hero video (treated as a bled photo, not a rounded glass card); reads the honest FAQ; understands NuzzlePal via spec-sheet-style rotated micro-labels on its real photo; picks a plan instantly via the plan tabs (no forced delay); can separately spin a demoted, clearly-labeled "spin for a bonus code" wheel purely as a side novelty, not the purchasing path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: quiet nav (small geometric logo mark + 4 plain text links, no pill CTA button) across the top; a giant all-caps condensed headline dominating the left ~60% of the hero; the real hero video bleeding to the frame's right/bottom edge with a small black "LIVE · NUZZLEPAL CAM" chip in the corner; one small solid-black pill CTA sitting quietly below the headline as the single primary action, with "Meet NuzzlePal" demoted to a plain text link beside it rather than a competing outline button.

FORM: user-pinned — https://dribbble.com/shots/25912049-Humanoid-AI-Robot-Company-Website ("Humanoid AI Robot Company Website" by Dannniel for Marcato Studio). A brief-pinned direction beats the roll per new-work.md; no concept-seed direction round was run for this build.
